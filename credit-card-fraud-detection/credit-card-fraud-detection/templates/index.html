Spot suspicious transactions before they cause damage.

Fraud Watch is a machine-learning demo that looks at the details of a transaction — amount, time, merchant type, device trust, and a few behavioural signals — and estimates how likely it is to be fraud. It's trained on a synthetic, anonymized dataset built for learning, not on real card numbers or personal data.

What is credit card fraud detection?

Credit card fraud detection is the process of identifying transactions that don't match a customer's usual spending behaviour — an unusually large purchase, a transaction from an unfamiliar location, or activity at an odd hour. Banks use statistical models to flag these transactions for review in real time. This project recreates that idea at a small scale: a supervised classification model learns patterns from labeled past transactions (normal vs. fraud) and applies them to new transactions to estimate a risk score.
